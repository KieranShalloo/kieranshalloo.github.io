A celebration of hope, heroism, and a galaxy far, far away — The Light Side is a lovingly
illustrated tribute to the
characters who fight for good. From wise old masters to rebel pilots and scrappy droids, this piece
brings together the
icons of the light side in a style that blends hand-drawn charm with digital screenprint textures.

The artwork began as a pencil and ink illustration, drawn by hand, before being coloured digitally
using a layered
technique that mimics traditional screenprinting. The result is bold and nostalgic — rich in
texture, full of subtle
details, and perfect for anyone who grew up dreaming of X-wings and lightsabers.

Printed at A3 size on beautifully smooth archival paper, it's ideal for hanging in a home office,
studio, or alongside
your collection of Star Wars prints, books or memorabilia.
