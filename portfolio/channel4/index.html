Brief

I was engaged at Channel 4 as a Principal-level Design Contractor to lead a full product redesign. My focus was on defining the visual, interaction, and motion foundations, including the creation of a tokenised motion design system. This work was intentionally aligned with the newly selected back-end technology stack and engineering partner to ensure feasibility, performance, and long-term scalability.

This project is under NDA and pending launch. As a result, this case study focuses on design leadership, problem framing, stakeholder alignment and decision-making rather than post-launch metrics.
