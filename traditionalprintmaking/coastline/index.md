The Coastline' is a hand-carved linocut relief print, designed and printed by hand inspired by the
landscape of
Portugal's rugged coastlibne.
This 160 x 230mm lino print is hand printed on Canaletto Velino paper using traditional Black oil-based
Cranfield
"Caligo Safe Wash" relief ink.

This print is a limited edition of 30 and is signed, numbered, and dated in pencil.
