'Forest’ is a hand-carved linocut print, designed and printed using traditional relief printmaking
techniques.
It’s part of
a printmaking series inspired by animals spotted while trail running through woodland paths inside Epping
forest just to the north of London.

Each print is hand-pulled using a blend of Phthalo Green oil-based Cranfield “Caligo Safe Wash” ink, and
printed onto
[insert paper type] paper.
