Lizard is a hand-carved single colour linocut relief print, inspired creatures seen on a family holiday
in Greece.

This 130 x 350mm print is hand printed on Fabriano Unica 250g paper using oil-based
Cranfield
"Caligo Safe
Wash" relief ink in Phthalo Green.

This print is a limited edition of 30 and is signed, numbered, and dated in pencil.
